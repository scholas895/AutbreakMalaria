# AutbreakMalaria — Malaria Outbreak Early Warning (Burundi)

**CPST-MalariaNet — Experiment 2 (Chapter 5)**

A reproducible pipeline for **district-level malaria outbreak early warning** in Burundi
using epidemiological (2022–2024), meteorological, and hydrological data.

The pipeline:

1. Ingests `BdiDATASETS.xlsx` (district-month cases + environmental covariates).
2. Computes **district-specific outbreak thresholds** (75th / 80th / 90th percentile of training cases).
3. Builds a **supervised early-warning task** at horizons **h = 1, 2, 3 months**.
4. Trains **6 classifiers**: Logistic Regression, Random Forest, XGBoost, LSTM-proxy (Gradient Boosting), TFT-proxy (Gradient Boosting), and a **probability-averaged Ensemble**.
5. Evaluates **discrimination** (AUROC, AUPRC), **calibration** (Brier, Log-Loss), **operational metrics** (Sensitivity, Specificity, False-Alarm Rate, Missed-Alert Rate), and **mean lead time**.
6. Exports **17 figures (PNG + PDF)** and **11 CSV tables** under `Chapter5_EWS_outputs/`.

## 7. Methodology (short version)

See **`docs/METHODOLOGY_NOTES.md`** for full details. Summary:

- **Label definition** — a district-month is an *outbreak* if `Cases > Q80(training)`; a *forecast example* at time `t` predicts `Outbreak_80(t+h)`.
- **Temporal split** — train: ≤ 2023-12-01 · val: 2024-01 → 2024-06 · test: > 2024-06-01.
- **Feature set** — 22 features: lags (1–3) of cases, rolling means (3, 6), case seasonality (sin/cos), environmental covariates + their lags.
- **Threshold selection** — per-model decision threshold selected on **validation F1**, then frozen for test.
- **Ensemble** — unweighted mean of base-model predicted probabilities.
- **Lead time** — mean months between first alert and observed outbreak within a 3-month look-back.
